# School fees, reconciliation and enrollment stop implementation plan

> For agentic workers: use Superpowers executing-plans and the final-only QA cadence in PLANS.md. Read the [product specification](../specs/product.md), [integration contracts](../specs/contracts.md) and [communications plan](2026-11-19-05-comms.md).

**Goal** Issue a distinct debt per child and billing month, give the named payer precise transfer details, account for actual externally verified payments, and process period-specific enrollment stops on iPhone/desktop web and Android APK. **Architecture / stack** Next.js on Vercel is the shared API, Supabase PostgreSQL stores private financial records and an auditable ledger, native Kotlin/Compose and Next web render the same server-confirmed state. The school verifies payments externally. Bank transfer is the baseline; recording cash is an optional operational method for schools that accept and document it.

**Planned files and shared constraints** `api/` means `apps/web/src/app/api/v1/`, `server/` means `apps/web/src/server/`, `web/` means `apps/web/src/features/`, `android/` means `apps/android/app/src/main/java/de/madrasty/app/features/` and `dto/` means `packages/contracts/src/`. Each listed API stem ends in `/route.ts`; routes are relative to `/api/v1`. Each SQL suffix below names a proposed ordered migration under `supabase/migrations/`, bound to an exact unused timestamp in F01. Extend the checked-in OpenAPI and both typed clients per card. RLS, F02 `commitCommand`, personal scoped reads and action-level capability checks apply at every boundary. Money uses integer minor units and school ISO currency; timestamps UTC, school dates YYYY-MM-DD and fee periods YYYY-MM. Show translated AR/DE field errors, stale/offline states and server confirmation on web and APK.

**Verification rule** Each card ends with inspection of the stated paths, permission rules, SQL constraints, ledger/event invariants and proposed final cases, then `pnpm check:types`, `pnpm build:web` and `pnpm build:android` as applicable. Log build results and outstanding final QA cases in PROGRESS.md. All test authoring and running starts at Q01 after the entire implementation and its isolated Supabase/Vercel deployment and signed staging APK exist. Q03/Q05/Q06/Q07 exercise the cases below with synthetic financial and family records.

**Review focus** Two guardians can trigger duplicate child debt. Two operators might spend the same receipt concurrently. An unmatched amount can make an invoice look paid. A new bank destination can be mistaken for the old one. A stop for one child can accidentally affect a sibling or erase money received. Each is covered below.

### B01 — Single active collection account and stable child transfer reference

**Requirements / depends** R13. F02, F03, I01, I02, I03, S01, S02; requires capability-granted administration, strong reauthentication, school currency, stable student number and display name. **Scope** Bank setup/change and data used on every payment instruction. Live account values and tariff come from the school before production.

**Files** `dto/bank.ts`, `server/finance/bank.ts`, migration `finance_bank_reference`, `api/finance/bank`, `web/finance/BankSettings.tsx`, `android/finance/BankSettings.kt`, `web/finance/TransferDetails.tsx`, `android/finance/TransferDetails.kt`.

**Contract** `GET /finance/bank` is scoped to bank administrators. `PUT /finance/bank {beneficiary,iban,revision,stepUpProof}` returns active version ID, effective time and masked audit summary. Validate normalized IBAN length/country format and checksum server-side; present beneficiary and normalized IBAN for explicit confirmation. Bind a fresh, one-use step-up proof to actor/session, purpose and confirmed proposed change. Store immutable, versioned bank records, a singleton active pointer and audited actor/time/old-new version IDs in one transaction. Only explicitly bank-authorized admins may change it. Expose `paymentInstructions(invoice,actor)` through B02's scoped invoice detail and `transferReference(studentId)` to B02; create once from child's name + immutable student number, freeze the normalized readable string for subsequent invoices and name changes.

- [ ] Add singleton bank settings, version history, unique stable per-child transfer references and school setup readiness in PostgreSQL. An unconfigured account blocks issuance that needs instructions. Protect actual IBAN and names from logs and broad audit views.
- [ ] Implement settings with preview, strong reauthentication, revision-checked commit, tamper/replay protection and audit entry. Serialize concurrent replacements, restrict edits to named capability, and retain versions needed to reproduce previously issued documents.
- [ ] Build reusable transfer display for both clients: beneficiary, IBAN, exact amount/currency and Verwendungszweck each have their own copy button and selectable fallback. Isolate Latin digits/IBAN/reference in RTL. Copy plain logical strings, without decorative separators, currency-formatting surprises or bidi control characters in IBAN. Show current confirmed instructions and a visible bank-change notice on open invoices; retain the issue-time snapshot for history and paid receipts.

**Acceptance / final cases** An accountant without bank-edit grant is refused. Expired proof, altered payload, if-match collision and replay cannot activate a second bank. Amount and reference can be copied independently in both languages. Two same-name children have distinguishable stable references. An issued unpaid invoice clearly identifies the currently valid recipient after a change; its original bank record remains reproducible. **DoD** Shared checkpoint plus single-active invariant, MFA binding and RTL copy-field inspection.

### B02 — Monthly issuance, manual charges and payer invoice detail

**Requirements / depends** R13. B01, S02, S03, I01. Requires existing child/guardian links and effective enrollment. This card provides the staff configuration path for a tariff and a named payer per child. Use synthetic tariff and payer data in staging. **Scope** Issue, list and read debts for one child each, including authorized batch preview/commit and genuinely independent manual items. Payment recording belongs to B03.

**Files** `dto/invoices.ts`, `server/finance/{invoices,billingEligibility,feeSettings}.ts`, migration `finance_invoices`, `api/finance/tariffs`, `api/finance/children/[id]/payer`, `api/finance/invoice-previews`, `api/finance/invoice-batches`, `api/finance/invoices`, `api/finance/invoices/[id]`, `web/finance/{FeeSettings,IssueInvoices,InvoiceDetails}.tsx`, `android/finance/{FeeSettings,IssueInvoices,InvoiceDetails}.kt`.

**Settings contracts** Authorized finance staff use `GET|POST /finance/tariffs` to read/create an effective-month tariff version with labeled fee lines, integer unit amounts, school currency and dueDay (1–31). Resolve the due day in the billed month, clamping to the last calendar day. Preview shows the selected tariff, default due date and any authorized override before issue. `GET|PUT /finance/children/{id}/payer` reads/sets `{guardianId,effectiveFromMonth,revision}` after selecting a currently approved guardian for that child; constrain one effective payer for any child/period. An approved import suggestion may use the same designation service. Reject an unapproved/revoked candidate; re-selection affects future issues and preserves each issued invoice snapshot.

**Invoice contract** `POST /finance/invoice-previews {period,classId?,studentIds?,kind:'monthly'|'manual',chargeSourceId?,lineItems?,dueDate?}` returns preview ID, per-child payer/line items/totals, existing invoices, ineligible/blocked rows, reason and version. `POST /finance/invoice-batches {previewId,revision}` returns issued/existing/skipped IDs and counts. Use the same workflow for an individual child. `GET /finance/invoices?period=&studentId=&state=&cursor=` and `GET /finance/invoices/{id}` return authorized invoice number, child, period, line items, due date, currency, total/allocated/balance, payer, payment instructions, receipt links and updated status. `billingEligibility(tx,studentId,period)` reads effective enrollment and later B05 stop guards. `invoice.issued` payload contains invoiceId, studentId, payerId, period, dueDate, revision; no bank instructions.

- [ ] Implement scoped fee settings, prospective version/date validation, payer selection and a settings preview on both platforms. Missing configuration opens the permitted settings action. Audit changes and display their effective month.
- [ ] Store invoices, mandatory named payer and snapshots of child, beneficiary/bank version, transfer reference, tariff and line items. Unique invoice number; unique monthly debt by (studentId,period) irrespective of payer, class, batch or tariff. Give each billable item a stable charge-source identity, constrain reuse across manual/monthly invoices and reject attempts to reissue a monthly fee as a manual item. Allow a separate documented discretionary charge for that child. Keep canceled invoices and corrections in history.
- [ ] Resolve eligible pupils, current payer, positive minor-unit amounts, single currency and applicable tariff on the server. Preview identifies omissions/conflicts explicitly. On confirmation lock affected child/periods in stable order and recompute eligibility, payer, tariff, bank and due snapshot. Return a stale-preview conflict for materially changed rows and re-preview before commit. Concurrent issuance with the same or independent keys can create at most one monthly debt. Persist invoice lines, batch receipt and outbox atomically.
- [ ] Build staff preview/commit/status and payer child/month list/detail on both clients. Surface a finance-only monthly queue of eligible children with no monthly issue yet; a month change opens the next period for authorized issuance. Schedule payer reminders from actually issued invoices, never from a draft batch. Restrict family visibility to named payer; retain that account's access to its own issued invoices after unlink, while removing school links and pending/future school alerts. Invoice inquiry routes to C01 accounts; copying details or reporting a transfer leaves the balance intact.

**Acceptance / final cases** A family with two children receives separate invoices/numbers/references; two guardians of one child cannot generate duplicate debt. Changing a tariff or payer after preview requires a new preview, while issued invoices retain their prior debtor and amounts. February clamps a configured day 31 to its actual final day. A late join, transfer, missing payer, duplicate charge, duplicate tap, concurrent batch and stale bank preview have explicit outcomes. An unrelated linked guardian cannot derive amount from search, export, receipts or detail. Historic named payer may reopen an invoice after unlink. **DoD** Shared checkpoint plus uniqueness/authorization and preview-commit mapping.

### B03 — Manual receipts, allocation, exceptions and reversals

**Requirements / depends** R13. B02. **Scope** Record verified full cash/bank payments, hold discrepant or unmatched receipts, allocate across debts and reverse incorrect postings. Online payment initiation, bank pulling and automated matching are outside the product.

**Files** `dto/receipts.ts`, `server/finance/{receipts,allocations}.ts`, migration `finance_receipt_ledger`, `api/finance/receipts`, `api/finance/receipts/[id]`, `api/finance/receipts/[id]/allocations`, `api/finance/receipts/[id]/reversals`, `web/finance/{RecordPayment,Reconciliation,ReceiptDetails}.tsx`, `android/finance/{RecordPayment,Reconciliation,ReceiptDetails}.kt`.

**Contract** `POST /finance/receipts {externalReceiptRef,method:'cash'|'bank',receivedDate,currency,amountMinor,invoiceId?,verified:true}` returns receipt ID/number, unallocated amount, invoice balances and status. A verified exact outstanding amount for one invoice may post in that transaction; other amounts enter `needs_matching` with zero automatic allocations. Use a bank-statement transaction reference or unique cash-voucher identity for externalReceiptRef; two identical amounts/names/dates alone do not establish duplication. `POST /finance/receipts/{id}/allocations {revision,lines:[{invoiceId,amountMinor,invoiceRevision}]}` returns posted lines and remainder. `POST /finance/receipts/{id}/reversals {revision,kind:'unapply'|'void_receipt',allocationIds?,reason}` creates compensating entries. If the optional cash-recording method is disabled, suppress it in the UI and reject a cash command server-side. `GET /finance/receipts?state=&cursor=` is the assigned finance queue; `GET /finance/receipts/{id}` projects only attributable allocations for each permitted payer, including after an unlink. Events `payment.recorded`, `payment.reversed` contain receipt/revision, affected invoice/allocation IDs and payer IDs. C07 groups one confirmation per relevant recipient for that committed posting.

- [ ] Store immutable receipt, credited allocation, reversing entries, external receipt business key and audit. Lock receipt and invoice rows in a common order; recompute remaining receipt funds and live invoice balance inside the transaction. Require positive amounts and same currency, reject over-allocation, wrong scope, canceled debts and already-reversed entries. Guard both Idempotency-Key and external reference; changed amount under an existing reference requires correction.
- [ ] Derive active received amount = verified receipt minus receipt voids; net applied = allocations minus their reversals; remainder = active received minus net applied. Invoice balance = collectible charge minus net applied. Status open/partial/paid follows actual balance. Unapply returns money to the unmatched remainder; voiding a mistaken receipt reverses active allocations and the original receipt together. For a real retained payment use unapply, not void. Preserve actor/date/reason, original receipt and corrected document. Never imply an external refund from a bookkeeping entry.
- [ ] Build staff invoice selection by child/month, confirmed external cash/bank entry, manual split preview, unallocated remainder queue and a reasoned reversal with before/after balances. Both clients show official receipts and corrections. A split spanning two families creates one internal receipt and private per-payer receipts/projections without disclosing unrelated debts.

**Acceptance / final cases** With two EUR 60 invoices and one verified EUR 100 transfer, both balances stay 60 until allocated. Allocating 60 + 30 yields paid/30 outstanding and EUR 10 unmatched. Unapplying the 30 restores that invoice's balance to 60 and raises remainder to 40. An independently retried reversal cannot debit twice. Two cash entries with distinct voucher IDs can have identical amounts; a repeated bank statement reference cannot count twice. A guardian tapping “transferred” cannot settle a bill. **DoD** Shared checkpoint plus invariants and immutable journal/export fields inspected.

### B04 — Scoped finance dashboards, statements and CSV

**Requirements / depends** R13. B02, B03. **Scope** Operational/accounting views of billed, received, allocated, reversed, outstanding, overdue and unmatched money; family views limited to their own named obligations. Administrative office case/search rights alone confer no financial export access.

**Files** `dto/financeReports.ts`, `server/finance/reports.ts`, migration `finance_reporting`, `api/finance/reports/summary`, `api/finance/reports/statement`, `api/finance/reports/export`, `web/finance/FinanceReports.tsx`, `android/finance/FinanceReports.kt`.

**Contract** `GET /finance/reports/summary?period=&classId=&asOf=` returns integer minor-unit totals grouped by currency, counts and matching/overdue queue links. `GET /finance/reports/statement?period=&studentId=&status=&cursor=&asOf=` returns scoped child/invoice/receipt rows with posting, reversal and remaining balances. `GET /finance/reports/export?period=&classId=&status=&asOf=` requires finance-export capability, parameters shared with the on-screen query, `text/csv; charset=utf-8`, attachment and private/no-store. Define invoice period vs received date as separately labeled filters; reconstruct the specified cutoff from immutable posting dates/times. Reuse actual ledger arithmetic and exclude cancelled charges from collectible totals.

- [ ] Build reporting queries over invoices and posted allocations. Sum each receipt once, including its remaining unmatched amount; keep received and applied measures distinct. Use historical enrollment on the billed period for class reports. Show named-payer statements limited to owned rows, private references/cross-payer allocations projected safely. The staff report and export use the same scoped predicate and cutoff.
- [ ] Build filterable dashboards, count-to-detail links, outstanding/matching work queues, paginated child statements and scoped export on web and APK. Quote/escape CSV, neutralize formula-like text, preserve stable student numbers, isolate RTL identifiers. Audit actor/time/filter/count for downloads. Expired permission prevents retrieval even with a copied link.

**Acceptance / final cases** Totals reconcile to B03's split example before and after reversal, including unmatched funds. An as-of export predating the reversal still shows the old state. Screen/export agree at identical cutoff, CSV cells with formula prefixes are safe, disabled export capability denies even an account that can view an invoice. Each sibling and payer has an independent statement. **DoD** Shared checkpoint plus report-to-ledger mapping and export grants.

### B05 — Child-specific requests to stop next month and financial reconciliation

**Requirements / depends** R14, R13, R11. B02, B03, B04, S02, S03, I01, F03. Requires effective-dated enrollment transaction/roster, fee period guard and approved parent-child link. **Scope** Parent request, withdrawal before decision, student affairs/admin queue, authorized decision and reconciliation. A separate invoice inquiry or daily absence never initiates a stop.

**Files** `dto/enrollmentStops.ts`, `server/finance/{stops,stopReconciliation}.ts`, extend `server/finance/{billingEligibility,receipts}.ts` and S03 enrollment service, migration `finance_enrollment_stops`, `api/enrollment-stops`, `api/enrollment-stops/[id]`, `api/enrollment-stops/[id]/decision`, `api/finance/stop-reviews`, `api/finance/stop-reviews/[id]`, `web/finance/{StopRequest,StopQueue,StopReview}.tsx`, `android/finance/{StopRequest,StopQueue,StopReview}.kt`.

**Contract** `POST /enrollment-stops {studentId,lastAttendanceMonth,duration:'one_month'|'indefinite',reason}` returns request ID, startMonth = lastAttendanceMonth + 1 calendar month, endMonthExclusive = start + 1 or null, request state and revision. Validate future start relative to the school clock, positive/real enrollment and nonoverlap for this child. `GET /enrollment-stops?status=&studentId=&cursor=` returns permitted family or staff queue. `PATCH /enrollment-stops/{id} {action:'withdraw',revision}` by submitting linked guardian while pending. `POST /enrollment-stops/{id}/decision {decision:'approve'|'reject',note?,revision}` by admin/student affairs. On submission, withdrawal and decision, create localized parent-facing notice intents. Limit private reason/note to requester and assigned staff. Accountants see finance review facts only. `GET /finance/stop-reviews` and `PATCH /finance/stop-reviews/{id} {resolution,receiptOrAdjustmentRef,reason,revision}` require finance capability. Resolution references an actual recorded adjustment/reallocation or documented externally confirmed refund as appropriate; it never moves money itself.

- [ ] Store interval-scoped stop records and decisions. States pending → withdrawn/rejected/approved. Use common child/period mutation locks for issue, decision and payment; protect overlapping requests, including multiple guardians and independent keys. Mark reminders on affected child/periods held immediately while pending. Withdrawal/rejection releases the hold and schedules only still-eligible reminders; prior months and siblings are untouched.
- [ ] On approval apply effective-dated S03 enrollment pause/end and billing exclusion in the same transaction as the approved stop; publish `enrollment.stop_approved` only after its effects commit. A one-month pause ends at the following month boundary, restoring original enrollment only where it independently remains valid; an indefinite stop has no automatic end. Preserve past attendance, reports and invoice history. Newly issued or concurrently generated in-period debts must see the same guard.
- [ ] For covered monthly enrollment charges, skip unissued debt, cancel open wholly unpaid invoice with audit/reason, and place any paid/partially paid invoice in finance review with original receipts and allocations intact. Mark reason for any independent manual charge and review its applicability separately. Halt associated reminders throughout review. The review owns signed charge-adjustment records linked to the original invoice and any recorded external-refund evidence. Cap adjustments at the original collectible amount, lock all affected invoices/receipts, and use B03 unapply/reallocation for reassignment or a recorded outward refund entry keyed by verified external reference. Reject duplicate resolutions and any disposition exceeding the current available funds. Update the reports' net-received/refund and collectible-charge measures in the same feature; preserve original documents. A review cannot close while retained money lacks a documented disposition. Re-derive reports and jobs after decision. Never auto-refund or silently erase received funds.
- [ ] Build child/month picker, explicit last attendance month/start/end, reason, review/confirm, status and withdraw on both clients. Build staff queue, scoped reason, effective period, approve/reject and finance escalation. Show successful submission only after server commit and notify the requester of decision/withdrawal. Emit `enrollment.stop_requested|enrollment.stop_withdrawn|enrollment.stop_rejected|enrollment.stop_approved` with request ID, student ID, requester, period boundaries and revision; omit reason from events, email and lock screens.

**Acceptance / final cases** Parent pays Ahmed externally, requests a one-month stop for Omar in December with November as the last attendance month: Ahmed's receipt stays valid, Omar's December reminders pause, November is unchanged and January resumes if independently enrolled. Try indefinite stop over a year boundary, two guardians submitting overlapping intervals, withdrawal racing approval, batch issuance concurrent with stop approval, partial payment arriving concurrently and correction following a full payment. After approval one unissued period is skipped, wholly open debt is cancelled, paid/partial debt retains ledger and finance review. Staff sees one decision, the appropriate guardian sees one notice and only the affected child/period changes. **DoD** Shared checkpoint plus enrollment/billing/reminder transaction map, finance review closure and final assertions in Q03/Q05.

## Binding platform-native frontend requirement (owner, 2026-11-25)
- iOS PWA: Apple HIG-style grouped lists, segmented controls, rounded/capsule buttons, compact symbol actions, contextual menus/sheets, safe areas and system typography where supported. It remains a PWA, not a SwiftUI binary. Do not make a new iOS target without a separate architecture decision.
- Android: Material 3 Jetpack Compose controls, Material list items, filter chips, menus and bottom sheets; use actual native components and Android interaction patterns. Do not copy iOS styles blindly onto Android.
- All screens: avoid sharp desktop-web-style form controls, giant secondary action buttons, excess cards and redundant text; keep the school's color identity, Arabic RTL/German LTR, light/dark, keyboard and reduced-motion support. Minimum target 44pt iOS / 48dp Android. Verify behavior and actual screen captures per platform before release. Apply to new work and touched screens; do not silently change unrelated workflows or permissions.
